Detailed Breakdown

Sales Rep Productivity

$385,320

Sellers currently move a deal across SAP, SharePoint, and email — re-keying the same information and rebuilding quotes that already exist somewhere. Consolidating onto a single opportunity record returns hours to the selling week. Only half the reclaimed time is counted as converting into real selling activity; the rest is assumed to absorb into the day.

38 reps6.0 admin hrs / week$65 / hr loaded50% recapture
38 × 6.0 × 52 × $65 × 0.50 = $385,320

Campaign & Trade Show ROI

$64,800

SPATCO runs roughly 30 trade shows a year with leads captured on badge scanners and manual spreadsheets. A measurable share of that investment is lost because leads are never worked. Marketing Cloud journeys and campaign influence reporting recover that share. This figure deliberately excludes the larger strategic value of being able to cut events that do not return.

30 shows / year$12,000 avg event spend18% yield lift
30 × $12,000 × 0.18 = $64,800

Onboarding & Ramp Time

$144,000

Without a CRM, new sellers ramp by shadowing tenured reps until the process clicks. A guided pipeline in Sales Cloud shortens that curve. This matters disproportionately under the $700–800M plan, where sales headcount is expected to grow faster than tribal knowledge can transfer.

12 hires / year4 weeks of ramp removed$3,000 / week loaded
12 × 4 × $3,000 = $144,000

Cross-Sell Whitespace

$664,200

With 80–90% of new business coming from existing relationships, the account base is heavily single-threaded — customers buying one line of business when SPATCO sells four or five. Data Cloud Foundations unifies profiles so those gaps become visible to reps, converting a relationship-led motion into a deliberate expansion motion. The single largest lever in the model, and the one most directly tied to the growth mandate.

900 commercial accounts82% single-threaded2.0% conversion$45,000 avg expansion deal
900 × 0.82 × 0.02 × $45,000 = $664,200
