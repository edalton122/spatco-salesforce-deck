01 — Systems

Fragmented Quoting

Sales activity lives in SAP, SharePoint, and email at the same time. Sales, sales support, and estimating each log into different places to move one deal forward — and no single record holds the whole story.

3disconnected systems per quote (SAP · SharePoint · email)
0shared opportunity records across the three teams
Hover →
Sales Cloud

One opportunity record. One source of truth.

  • Every quote, contact, and activity attaches to a single opportunity — not a folder or an inbox.
  • Sales, sales support, and estimating work the same record with role-appropriate visibility.
  • SAP stays the system of record — Salesforce syncs with it as the front-end engagement layer.
Signed Phase 1 · Sales Cloud configuration
02 — Motion

Reactive-Only Sales

Roughly 80–90% of new business comes from existing relationships. There's no fit-scoring, no lead prioritization, and reps have no visibility into account whitespace — so growth tracks the relationships, not the market.

80–90%of new business from existing relationships
Noneof the account base scored for fit or whitespace
Hover →
Data Cloud + Sales Cloud

See the whitespace. Then go get it.

  • Data Cloud Foundations unifies account profiles so every line of business on an account is visible in one place.
  • Reps can see which accounts buy one service and not the other four — and prioritize outreach accordingly.
  • Relationship-led selling stays — it just stops being the only motion available.
Signed Phase 1 · Sales Cloud + Data Cloud Foundations

No Campaign Attribution

Marketing Cloud

Every show lead, traced to pipeline.

  • Campaign influence reporting answers "what did that show return?" with a number, not a guess.

Product Demo

From badge scan to booked meeting —
without a spreadsheet in the middle.

The single loop that closes the Slide 2 gap: a trade-show lead becomes a CRM record and gets followed up automatically. Three steps, no manual hand-offs.

1

The lead is captured at the booth

A badge scan at one of SPATCO's ~30 annual shows writes straight into a Marketing Cloud campaign — tagged to the event, the booth, and the rep who scanned it. No badge-scanner export, no overnight spreadsheet cleanup.

Marketing Cloud
2

It becomes a real CRM record

The lead lands in Sales Cloud with the account already matched against unified Data Cloud profiles — so the rep immediately sees whether this is a net-new prospect or an existing customer buying a second line of business.

Sales Cloud + Data Cloud
3

Follow-up happens on its own

An automated journey goes out within hours while SPATCO is still fresh. Rep activity, quote, and outcome all attach to one opportunity — which is what finally makes "what did that show return?" a reportable number.

Campaign Influence Reporting

Why this loop first: it is the shortest path from a real SPATCO pain point to a number Scott and John can see on a dashboard. Nothing here depends on AI, and nothing here touches SAP's role as the system of record.

Platform Architecture

A foundation first.
Intelligence only where it earns its place.

Phase 1 is a data and engagement foundation, not an AI program. The trust layer is here because SPATCO's IT team asked the right question early: does proprietary data ever train someone else's model?

The Path Forward

Where this takes you.
